Translation workbench

Properties that still need a translation, with what they say in a language you read. What you write is collected in your batch and exported to QuickStatements; WDProp makes no edit itself.

Keyboard

While the cursor is in a translation field: Enter adds it to the batch and moves to the next property, Esc skips the property, and Alt+↑/↓ moves without doing either. Skipped properties stay hidden on this browser until you show them again.
