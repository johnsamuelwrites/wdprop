My contributions

What you have exported to QuickStatements, and what Wikidata says about it now. WDProp cannot see whether a batch was run, so it simply looks up each proposal and reports what is there.

A proposal shown as not there may mean the batch was never run, that QuickStatements rejected the line, or that the edit was reverted. You can put any of them back in your batch and try again. This history is kept in this browser only.
