Translation batch

Translations you have proposed are kept in this browser until you export them. WDProp produces QuickStatements commands, which you run yourself, under your own account.

To add translations, use the translation workbench, which lists the properties still missing one. You can also use ＋ next to a property on a language page, or open a property and choose Propose a translation.
